# 📊 Amazon Prime Video Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** built using an Amazon Prime Video dataset. It provides insights into content distribution, ratings, genres, release trends, and global availability of shows and movies on the platform.

The dashboard is designed to help users quickly understand content patterns and trends through visually engaging charts and KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze the distribution of movies and TV shows
* Understand content ratings and audience targeting
* Identify popular genres
* Explore global content distribution
* Track content growth over time

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard creation
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and preprocessing

---

## 📂 Dataset Features

The dataset includes:

* Show ID
* Title
* Type (Movie / TV Show)
* Director
* Cast
* Country
* Release Year
* Rating
* Genre (Listed In)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 🔢 Key Metrics (KPIs)

* **Total Titles:** 9655
* **Total Ratings:** 25
* **Total Genres:** 519
* **Total Directors:** 5771
* **Content Timeline:** 1920 – 2021

---

### 📊 Visualizations

#### 1. Ratings by Total Shows

* Shows are categorized based on ratings like **13+, 16+, 18+, PG-13, etc.**
* Helps understand audience segmentation

#### 2. Genres by Total Shows

* **Drama** and **Comedy** dominate the platform
* Other categories include Documentary, Kids, and Animation

#### 3. Movies vs TV Shows

* **Movies:** 81%
* **TV Shows:** 19%
* Indicates a strong focus on movie content

#### 4. Total Shows by Release Year

* Significant growth after the year 2000
* Rapid increase in recent years showing platform expansion

#### 5. Total Shows by Country

* Major contributions from countries like:

  * United States
  * India
* Highlights global distribution of content

---

## 🌍 Key Insights

* The platform is heavily dominated by **movies over TV shows**
* **Drama and Comedy** are the most popular genres
* Content production has **grown exponentially in recent years**
* A few countries contribute the majority of content
* Most shows fall under **teen and adult ratings**

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the `.pbix` file in **Power BI Desktop**
2. Use filters and slicers to explore:

   * Genre
   * Country
   * Release Year
3. Hover over visuals for detailed insights
